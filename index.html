Molecular Biology Paradoxes: The Limits of DNA

"What amazes me most isn't what DNA achieves; it's what it is incapable of doing. DNA cannot move a molecule, generate energy, or read itself. Letters don't act — they describe. You need a ribosome to read DNA, but DNA codes the ribosome. In biology, 'almost working' is death; life begins fully functional."
